# D · THE DUGA RADAR

## The idea

He walks past the Duga over-the-horizon radar array in the forest and never looks at it. It is the
clearest single expression of the character in the whole film: a man who does not react to anything.
The structure is enormous, silent, and he passes it the way you pass a lamp post on your own street.

## The two problems, and why it was set aside

**1. It is a spoiler.** Duga is instantly recognisable. Show it before the city sign and the audience
already knows where they are — the reveal at ПРИПЯТЬ dies. The prompt below solves this by showing only
a fragment: one lattice leg and a section of mesh, trees occluding it on all sides, the full silhouette
never visible. What gives it away is the profile, not the object.

**2. The geography does not line up.** Duga sits about **10 km south** of the power plant. Pripyat is
about **3 km north** of it. That is 13–15 km apart, on opposite sides, and walking past Duga means the
city sign is still half a day away past the plant itself.

This is survivable — the film cuts freely and never claims continuous geography — but **only if the Duga
shot is not adjacent to the sign.** If it is used at all, it belongs at the very start, with the whole
forest walk between it and the sign, so it reads as "somewhere early in a long walk" rather than "a minute
before arriving".

## Where it would go

```
D1   Duga            — he passes the structure
01   Forest          — the long walk, out onto the road
02   Arrival         — ПРИПЯТЬ
```

## The other option, if this one is dropped

Put Duga on the horizon of the aerial panorama (04A) instead, alongside the confinement arch — one line
in that prompt, no extra generation. It costs nothing and it is after the reveal, so it spoils nothing.
The reason it was not done: 04A is a wide, calm, even shot, and a recognisable landmark in it becomes the
one place in the film where the camera says "look at this". The whole picture is built on nothing being
singled out.
