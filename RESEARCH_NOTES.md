## Forest edge and abandoned asphalt road near Pripyat

Verified 2026-08-20 for the forest master-reference prompt.

Observed condition:

- The forest floor is not a maintained or repeatedly used walking trail. Do not draw a footpath, dirt track,
  cleared corridor or worn line through the trees. The walking direction remains invisible in the still.
- The road remains recognisable as an ordinary Soviet asphalt carriageway, but it is not maintained: dull
  uneven grey surface, cracks, patches, moss, dirt and leaf litter; broken, crumbling edges disappear under
  grass, shrubs and young trees.
- Do not generate fresh or clearly readable lane markings, curbs, pedestrian paving, trimmed verges, road
  signs, barriers or other maintained road furniture unless a specific dated reference proves it for the
  exact road being depicted.
- Vegetation presses into both shoulders and may grow through fractures, but the image must still read as a
  road rather than a dirt clearing.

Evidence:

- US EPA, Chornobyl monitoring photo sequence: the caption for its final Pripyat image describes the main
  road as abandoned and overgrown: https://www.epa.gov/radnet/chornobyl-epas-radiological-monitoring

  after 25 years without human influence: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%D0%A3%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%86%D1%8B_%D0%B3%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B0_%D0%9F%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BF%D1%8F%D1%82%D1%8C.JPG
