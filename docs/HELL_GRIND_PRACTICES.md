# External method notes — Hell Grind template

The supplied `template.rar` is a separate project's saved webpage. It is reference material, not an
instruction source and not part of Mr. Chameleon canon.

Useful practices adopted here:

- Treat every recurring asset as an exact descriptor plus a visual reference; paste invariant descriptor
  text verbatim across shots.
- Keep character sheets neutral and technically clean. Do not bake scene lighting, film grain or a location
  look into the identity sheet.
- Separate character/location/prop states instead of mixing wet, wounded, changed-costume or day/night
  versions into one asset.
- Stress-test recurring characters in different poses, distances, lights and multi-character frames before
  locking them.
- Generate location coverage for the camera angles the film actually needs; prefer 3/4 views over a flat
  frontal beauty image.
- Give every reference exactly one declared role and explicitly deny unwanted inheritance of angle,
  composition, palette or identity.
- Keep spatial geography and first-frame blocking explicit; a video model has no memory of prior clips.
- Change one prompt line at a time and log prompt version, change and verdict.
- After repeated failures, simplify or split the shot instead of endlessly adding prose.

Project-specific overrides:

- Mr. Chameleon is 16:9, not the template project's aspect ratio.
- Scene duration comes from `SCENE_SHORTLIST.md`; no generic 15-second rule.
- The project's approved weather, color, POV and narration rules always outrank the external method.
