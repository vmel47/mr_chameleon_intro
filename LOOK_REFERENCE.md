## Look distilled from the approved footage

- Late spring turning into early summer, full fresh birch/poplar green.
- 1:00 PM under half-broken cloud.
- High soft sun three-quarters behind; restrained warm rim, never a warm overall filter.
- Neutral daylight white balance around 5600K.
- Concrete stays neutral weathered grey; it must not drift blue, cyan, green or sepia.
- Foliage is naturally saturated new-growth green, not emerald, teal or autumn yellow.
- Sky is pale blue with retained cloud detail and no clipped white mass.
- Medium contrast. Midtones remain open; shadows are dark but readable and never crushed.
- Light atmospheric haze with distance; no exaggerated bloom or fog.
- Even exposure corner to corner: no vignette, edge darkening or lens shading.
- Documentary cinematic realism. No teal-and-orange grade, bleach bypass, HDR halos or film-emulation LUT.

The three approved clips are not exposure-identical. Do not copy a single sampled frame literally. Preserve
their shared colour relationships and let exposure vary only as physically required by canopy, open yard,
or façade orientation.

The director supplied three views of the real Pripyat building. The first is preferred for its colour and
clear geometry; the second and third are authoritative for the current level of decay, missing glazing,
