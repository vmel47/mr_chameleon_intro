# Acting system — Mr. Chameleon adaptation

Use only for scenes with visible characters (`11–18`) or when building character assets. Part 1 (`01–10`)
does not need acting prose for the invisible POV walker; it needs embodied camera physics instead.

## Core method

- Acting is behavior under pressure, not an emotion label.
- Give each recurring character one master performance profile: objective engine, posture, gait, breath,
  voice identity, signature behavior with trigger, concealment behavior and the condition that cracks it.
- Rewrite the profile for the scene; do not paste every trait into every shot.
- For each beat define objective, obstacle, tactic and the visible behavior change when the tactic fails.
- Eyes lead thought: natural gaze targets, micro-saccades, state-dependent blinks and live catchlights. Chosen
  stillness is not frozen eyes.
- Give hands and body a truthful physical task. A stopped task can punctuate a beat.
- Reactions begin while information is being received. Ensemble reactions travel in a wave, not in sync.
- Preserve state inertia across cuts; do not reset fatigue, fear, injury or status.
- Voice identity is locked verbatim across scenes. Narrator voice remains outside video-generation prompts.

Write only observable, filmable behavior. Keep performance inside the motion/acting block; costume, camera,
lighting and grade belong elsewhere. Consult `references/SOURCE_ACTING.md` for the full supplied craft notes.
