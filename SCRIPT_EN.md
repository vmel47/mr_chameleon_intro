# MR. CHAMELEON — SHOOTING SCRIPT (EN)

**Format:** 16:9 · **Runtime:** 4:16 · **Clips:** 28 · **Max clip length:** 20s
**Setting:** the Chernobyl exclusion zone — the forest approach, Pripyat, one residential block, one basement.
**Sound:** environmental only. Narration recorded in post (see `NARRATION.md`). One human voice in the entire film — the scream in the final clip.

**The spine.** A man walks out of the forest into a city that has been empty for forty years, and gives a calm guided tour of his home. Nothing threatens him. Nothing happens to him. The tension is entirely in the fact that he is comfortable. The film withholds his face for four minutes, shows it once, and then explains — in a basement, in a mirror — what he actually is.

**Point of view.** Most of the film is first person. The camera is a body, not a tripod: breath, head-turn latency, the small settle after each turn. His hands and feet stay out of frame throughout — he is never physically established until the reveal.

### 26 · TO THE MIRROR — 8s — 3:52

Camera behind `@char_MC_employee` as he works. A slow push past him toward the mirror. In the glass, his reflection is clear and complete, head down.

> **VO-12 (3:53):** "That is when I started to change."

### 27 · THE DOUBLE — 8s — 4:00

Camera stopped. The mirror fills the frame. The worker is still at his table. From the side of the frame a second man walks into the reflection — `@char_MC_employee_civil`, exactly the same face, in ordinary clothes. Both are in the mirror at once. Neither reacts.

### 28 · THE SCREAM — 8s — 4:08

The worker lifts his head, sees his own copy, and screams — hands going to his head, the whole body folding into it.

> **VO-13 (4:12):** "But I am still good old me." *(beat)* "Or am I?"

## STRUCTURAL NOTES

**Why the runtime moved.** The original block breakdown ran 3:06. Three things needed the extra minute:

- **The panorama and the park have to breathe.** Abandonment only reads in long, patient frames. Cut them to five seconds and the city looks like set dressing.
- **The push-in at 3:14 is the whole film's payoff** and cannot be rushed. Twelve seconds of continuous approach, then six seconds of nothing but his face, is what makes the pupils land.
- **Several of your blocks contained two or three different locations**, which cannot be one generation. Splitting them added real time rather than padding.

**Where the seconds were spent.** Forest 16 → 26 (the sign now arrives at distance before it arrives close). City 36 → 56 (the panorama and two interiors). Park 20 → 26. Home 36 → 44. The face 20 → 26. Basement 36 → 44.

**Trim path if ever needed.** Clips 10, 11, 13, 14 are the only fully expendable ones — losing all four costs 28 seconds and returns the film to 3:48 without touching a single narration cue.

**The one continuity law of this film.** His face does not exist until 3:26. Every clip before it that contains his body must state, in POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS, that the face is turned away or out of frame. One leaked face in clip 08 or 16 kills the ending.
