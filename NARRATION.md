## DELIVERY NOTES

**VO-02 "Home, sweet home."** — the only line with anything like warmth in it. Do not smile through it; let the words do it.

**VO-05 "This is me."** — deliberately ambiguous. He is standing in front of a building with his back to camera, face unseen. Read it flat, as a plain statement of fact, not as an introduction.

**VO-06 / VO-07 / VO-08** — the house tour. Identical delivery, identical tempo, no build. Three lines that could be a man showing a guest around a flat he still lives in. The flatness is the whole point.

**VO-09 "They say the radiation changed me."** — lands on the first piece of the approach, then the film cuts away from him for the first time. Do not let the line run to the cut; leave a second of silence before the basement arrives.

**VO-13** — the last line of the film, and it plays entirely over the scream. "But I am still good old me." is closed and certain, spoken flat while a woman is coming apart underneath it. The pause is real, about a second. "Or am I?" is quieter, not louder — pitched down, almost to himself — and it finishes just before the cut to black. He is being casual about what he has just done to somebody. That gap is the whole character.
